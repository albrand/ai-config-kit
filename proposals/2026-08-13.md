## MERGE:
- **CLAUDE.md “Challenge directives and assumptions”** and **“Directive challenge”** repeat the same counterpart critique, source-precedence, and authorization requirements. Keep one concise rule.
- **AGENTS.md “Core operating principles”**, **“Validation truthfulness and completion standard”**, and **“Always report changes”** should share one completion contract rather than restating evidence, validation-state, and reporting requirements.
- **AGENTS.md “Delegated fast path”** and **“Running as a delegated executor”** encode the same packet, scope, validation, escalation, and close-out contract. Keep the trigger in `AGENTS.md`; move procedure to `OPENCODE_DELEGATION.md`.

## RETIRE:
- **CLAUDE.md “Multi-agent orchestration”**: retire “At intake find ≥1 bounded sidecar.” It is contradicted by **“One delegation rule”**: “no obligation to delegate when it adds nothing.” Mandatory speculative intake delegation also works against the measured cached-context dominance.
- **CLAUDE.md “Validate with Hermes before claiming done”**: retire the universal requirement for *every* non-trivial change. `cmd:bb` accounts for 47.4M attributed tokens, while **“Completion requires evidence”** already requires validation without mandating another model-mediated review.
- **AGENTS.md “Board-backed regression protection”**: retire the universal “Missing board access … is Blocked / NOT READY.” It is inapplicable outside board-backed repositories and contradicts **“Source-of-truth order”**, which makes repo-local criteria controlling.

## CONFLICT:
- **CLAUDE.md “Multi-agent orchestration”**: “At intake find ≥1 bounded sidecar” conflicts with **“One delegation rule”**: “no obligation to delegate when it adds nothing.”
- **CLAUDE.md “opencode delegation”**: “once architected, you delegate EXECUTION to opencode” conflicts with **“One delegation rule”** requiring delegation to be “proportional.”
- **AGENTS.md “Board-backed regression protection”** says missing board access is always “NOT READY,” while **“Source-of-truth order”** says repo-local rules and accepted criteria control repository details.
