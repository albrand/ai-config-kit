## CONFLICT
**AGENTS.md — Delegated fast path** requires “run at most one focused validation round” and budgets “2 validation calls.”

**AGENTS.md — Delivery first** requires “exercising the fix after” and says: “A known defect, or a check you can run, inside the work is done now, not listed.”

A delegated executor that discovers an in-scope defect during validation cannot consistently obey both: repairing it requires another validation round. **Delivery first** already wins by explicit precedence, so the validation ceilings are overridden instructions still consuming context and inviting premature handoffs.

Remove those ceilings. Retain focused validation, bounded scope, and **Delivery first**’s full-suite-on-final-candidate rule. This is distinct from the pending fast-path proposals about planning and unlisted skills.
